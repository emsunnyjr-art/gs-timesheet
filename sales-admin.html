Transfer Stock Between Branches

Internal movement of already-owned stock when a branch runs low and can't wait for a bulk supplier delivery — no supplier, no check, no payment. The receiving branch's WAC blends the incoming stock at the sending branch's most recent delivery cost (not their current blended WAC); the sending branch's own WAC is untouched, same as a sale.
